# Pure Scan Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-28

Pure Scan is a document scanner that works entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no servers, no ads and no analytics. Your documents never leave your device unless you choose to share them.

## Summary

- Pure Scan has no accounts, no backend server, no ads, and no analytics or crash-reporting SDK.
- The app's own code does not send anything over the network.
- Your scanned images, recognized text and PDFs are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.
- Your documents never leave your device, except when you explicitly share or save an export to a destination you choose.
- Google's ML Kit components, which power scanning and text recognition, are covered in their own section below.

## Data We Collect

Pure Scan does not collect personal information. The developer receives no data from the app: there is no account system, no server that receives your data, and no third-party advertising or analytics service built into the app.

Scanned pages, recognized text, document names and PDFs you create are kept on your device only, in storage that other apps cannot read. Deleting a document removes it from the app, and uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

## Camera Permission

Pure Scan asks for camera access so that you can scan document pages. The camera is used only when you start a scan. Photos are processed on your device and are not uploaded anywhere by the app.

## Your Documents Stay on Your Device

Your documents never leave your device. Images, recognized text and PDFs are processed and stored locally.

The only time content leaves the app is when you deliberately share or save a PDF or text export through the Android share sheet. In that case the content goes to the destination you pick, such as another app, email or a cloud drive, and that destination's own privacy policy applies from then on.

## Google ML Kit

Pure Scan uses two Google ML Kit components:

- **Text recognition** runs on your device to read the text in your scans.
- **The document scanner** is provided by Google Play services and shows Google's own scanning screen.

According to Google's ML Kit data disclosure, these components may send Google technical diagnostics, such as your device model, Android version, the app's package name and version, and performance measurements. This is Google's own processing under Google's terms; Pure Scan does not receive this information. Document images and recognized text are not part of what Google lists as collected. You can read Google's description here: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure

## Backups

Android automatic cloud backup is turned off for Pure Scan. Your scanned documents are therefore not copied to your Google account through Android's backup service. If you reinstall the app or move to a new phone, your documents do not come with you. Export anything you want to keep as a PDF first.

## Permissions

- **Camera**: used to scan document pages.
- **Internet and network state**: declared by the underlying framework and libraries. Pure Scan's own code does not use them to send your data anywhere.
- **Storage (Android 12L and below only)**: used only for saving exports on older Android versions.
- **Vibrate**: used for haptic feedback.

## Children

Pure Scan does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
